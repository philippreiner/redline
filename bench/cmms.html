Description

Hydraulic oil is leaking at the main cylinder rod seal of press HP-300, about 0.4 l per shift. Strokes were slow on the last 20 parts of the late shift.

Replace rod seal and wiper ring, check the rod surface for scoring, refill with ISO VG 46 and bleed the circuit. Run 20 test strokes before release to production.

Lockout/tagout required. Release accumulator pressure before opening the circuit. See LOTO procedure, press line 3.
